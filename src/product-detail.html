Product Description

Jameson Irish Whiskey is a blend of pot still and fine grain whiskeys that is triple-distilled to deliver exceptional smoothness. Perfect for any occasion, Jameson is best enjoyed with friends.

The world's best-selling Irish whiskey is exceptionally smooth and perfectly balanced with sweet wood and spicy tones. The rich heritage dates back to 1780 when the Jameson dynasty was founded in Dublin, and continues to thrive in the heart and souls of all who try it today.

Tasting Notes

  • Nose: Light floral fragrance, peppered with spicy wood and sweet notes
  • Taste: Perfect balance of spicy, nutty and vanilla with hints of sweet sherry
  • Finish: Smooth and mellow, with a pleasant lingering warmth

Serving Suggestion

Enjoy neat, on the rocks, with a dash of water, or in classic cocktails like the Whiskey Sour, Irish Coffee, or the refreshing Jameson, Ginger Ale & Lime.
